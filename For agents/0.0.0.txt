Technical Specification Document: 1v1 Virtual Card Game
1. System Architecture & Tech Stack Recommendations

To ensure a smooth, real-time, browser-based experience, the following stack is recommended for the programming agent:

    Frontend: React, Vue, or vanilla HTML5/TypeScript with a rendering library (e.g., PixiJS) or standard CSS Grid/Flexbox for card layouts.

    Backend: Node.js with Express or Python with FastAPI.

    Real-time Communication: WebSockets (using Socket.io) to handle turn synchronization, drafts, and actions between the two players.

    State Management: Redux, Zustand, or Pinia to maintain the complex board state, hand sizes, and active status effects.

2. Card Values and Base Attributes

Every card has two core stats: Attack (ATK) and Health (HP).

    Normal Cards (2 to 10): ATK and HP are both equal to the card's numerical value (e.g., a 6 has 6 ATK and 6 HP).

    Elite Cards (J, Q, K, A):

        Jack (J) = 11 ATK / 11 HP

        Queen (Q) = 12 ATK / 12 HP

        King (K) = 13 ATK / 13 HP

        Ace (A) = 14 ATK / 14 HP

3. Phase I: Deck-Building (Draft Phase)

The draft phase must occur before the gameplay begins.
3.1 Normal Card Drafting

    Draft Order (8 Subsections Total):

        A random player (Player A) is selected to pick first.

        Player A selects 1 subsection (e.g., Even Diamonds). Player B automatically receives the remaining subsection of that suit (e.g., Odd Diamonds).

        Player B drafts 2 subsections from the remaining suits (e.g., Even Spades, Odd Hearts). Player A automatically receives the paired subsections of those selected suits.

        Player A drafts 2 subsections from the remaining options. Player B receives the paired subsections.

        Player B drafts the remaining subsections. Player A receives the paired subsections.

    Result: Both players end up with exactly 4 subsections of normal cards (16 to 20 cards total, depending on the combination of Odd/Even subsections drafted).

5. Phase III: Game Initialization & Setup

    Starting HP: Both players begin with 150 Life Points (LP). Max LP is 150.

    Starting Hand: Each player draws 6 cards from their constructed deck.

        Constraint: This initial draw must not include any Elite cards. If an Elite card is picked during the initial draw, it must be shuffled back into its respective Elite slot, or the draw logic must skip Elite cards and only draw from the normal card pool for the starting hand.

    First Turn: Player A (who went first in the normal draft) starts.

6. Phase IV: Core Turn Loop

On each turn, the active player:

    Draws 1 card.

    Can choose to play a card (or multiple cards, depending on active buffs) or pass.

    Performs combat or activates card abilities.

    Ends turn. At the end of the turn, the cleanup phase restores all surviving cards on the board back to their maximum HP.

7. Suit Abilities (Normal Cards)

When a normal card is played, the player must select one of its two powers to activate (unless the "10-Card Hand" buff is active, which triggers both).
7.1 Diamonds (Каро)

    Power 1 (Draw): Immediately draw 1 additional card.

    Power 2 (Strike): The card gains "Haste/Charge" and can attack immediately on the turn it is played. (Normally, cards must wait until the next turn to attack).

7.2 Hearts (Купа)

    Power 1 (Heal): Heal a friendly card on the board or heal the player's LP. (See Section 9 for overheal mechanics).

    Power 2 (Damage): Deal direct damage to the opposing player's LP.

7.3 Spades (Пика)

    Power 1 (Tank): Position the card forward on the UI. The opponent is forced to attack and defeat this card before they can target any other friendly cards or the player's LP.

    Power 2 (Stun): Choose an enemy card. Rotate it 90 degrees. This card cannot attack during its owner's next turn.

7.4 Clubs (Спатия)

    Power 1 (Detonate): Deal damage equal to this card's current value to all enemy cards on the board. The card is then automatically destroyed and sent to the Defeated Pile.

    Power 2 (Resurrect Less): Summon a Clubs card from your Defeated Pile, but with strictly fewer damage/health points than its original state.

8. The "10-Card Hand" Buff State

If a player accumulates exactly 10 cards in their hand, they instantly gain a special buff state with the following rules:

    Draw Freeze: The player stops drawing a card at the beginning of their turn.

    Dual Activation: Every normal card played automatically activates both of its powers. (This does not apply to Elite cards).

    Play Limit: The player can play up to 3 cards per turn.

    Deactivation: The buff state is lost as soon as the player's hand size drops to 6 or fewer cards. Normal draw rules and single-power choices resume on the next turn.

10. Combat, Healing, and Advanced State Rules
10.1 Combat Resolution & Excess Damage

When Card X attacks Card Y:

    Card Y takes damage equal to Card X's ATK.

    Card X takes damage equal to Card Y's ATK.

    If a card's current HP falls to 0 or below, it is moved to the Defeated Pile.

    Excess Damage Rule: If Card X's ATK is greater than Card Y's remaining HP, the remaining damage is applied directly to the defending player's LP.

        Example: A 4-ATK card attacks a card with 2 remaining HP. The defending card is defeated, and 2 damage (

        4−2

              

        ) is dealt directly to the defending player's LP.

10.2 Overheal Rule

    The maximum player health is 150 LP.

    If a player is healed while at 150 LP (or the healing amount exceeds what is needed to reach 150), all excess healing is converted directly into damage against the opponent's LP.

10.4 Defeated Pile Drawing (Fatigue Phase)

    When a player's main deck becomes empty, they must draw cards from their Defeated Pile.

    This draw behaves as a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) queue (the first card that entered the Defeated Pile is the first one drawn).

    Fatigue Damage: Every card drawn from the Defeated Pile inflicts direct damage to the drawing player's LP. This damage starts at 1 and increases by 1 for each subsequent draw (1, 2, 3, etc.).
